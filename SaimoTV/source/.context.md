BrightScript source shared by the scene and tasks. `bslib.brs` contains pure parsers and helpers, and `main.brs` starts the Roku scene.

## Summary

- `bslib.brs` — URL helpers, HTTP, playlist and VOD parsers, Saimo catalog parsers, cache keys, search, subtitles, art application, and XMLTV time helpers.
- `main.brs` — Creates `roSGScreen`, opens `MainScene`, and waits until the screen closes.

## Constraints

- MUST keep `bslib.brs` independent of UI state and SceneGraph nodes.
- MUST keep the SceneGraph entry point named `Main`, as required by Roku.

## Guidance

- SHOULD add pure parser behavior to `bslib.brs` so `tools/tests/parsers_test.brs` can test it without a Roku device.
